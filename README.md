# NutriFit AI 🥗💪

**NutriFit AI** is an AI-powered personalized fitness and nutrition planner that generates tailored workout routines and diet plans based on your goals, preferences, and lifestyle. Whether your aim is weight loss, muscle gain, or overall wellness, NutriFit AI provides intelligent, adaptive recommendations to help you achieve your health objectives efficiently.

---

## Features

- **Personalized Workout Plans**  
  Generates exercise routines based on fitness level, goals, and available equipment. Includes strength training, cardio, flexibility, and recovery exercises.

- **Customized Meal Plans**  
  Offers diet plans that fit your nutritional needs, dietary restrictions, and preferences, ensuring balanced and enjoyable meals.

- **Progress Tracking**  
  Monitor achievements, track body metrics, and see improvements over time. AI adapts recommendations as your fitness evolves.

- **AI-Driven Insights**  
  Provides tips, motivational feedback, and intelligent suggestions to overcome plateaus and build healthy habits.

- **User-Friendly Interface**  
  Intuitive dashboards and clear instructions for a seamless experience.
